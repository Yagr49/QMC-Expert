Figure 3. Absolute prediction-error distributions for (a) cation holdout and (b) scaffold holdout. Each violin uses the mean absolute error across three runs (seeds 0–2) for each molecule–endpoint pair, giving 420 exact endpoint summaries per route and panel. Correlated endpoints and runs preclude interpreting density differences as statistical significance or causal human-label effects. These are not errors of ensemble predictions. White markers show medians and bars show interquartile ranges; shared linear axes retain all tails. Frozen head, LoRA and full FT start from pretrained MolFormer. Human direct and Human → CB share human-guided partial-backbone source checkpoints; the former further fine-tunes on targets, whereas the latter freezes source features and fits CatBoost. These human models are separate follow-ups, not the external v5 compact encoder.
