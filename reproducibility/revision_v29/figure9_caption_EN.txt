Figure 9. Predictive contribution of human supervision under matched controls. (a) External compact encoders: human minus continuous-MIC, shuffled-human or raw MolFormer errors. (b) Partial-backbone adaptation: human minus shuffled-human or raw MolFormer errors. Points show pooled ΔRMSE on log₂(mg/L); negative values favour human guidance. Lines are conditional 95% paired source-seed/group bootstrap intervals; hollow points mark intervals including zero. Five paired source runs are resampled with 103 cations for cation holdout, 29 scaffolds for scaffold holdout or 17 OOD cations. Compact and partial-backbone intervals use 10,000 and 5,000 resamples, respectively, with prediction models held fixed and no multiplicity adjustment. Cation-clustered intervals here differ from the scaffold-clustered sensitivity intervals quoted in the text. The architectures and decoders differ between panels; contrasts describe prediction procedures rather than biological causality or equivalence.
