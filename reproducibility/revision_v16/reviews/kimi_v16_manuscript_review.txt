• ## Review of v16 additions (direct prediction, LoRA/partial/full FT, weight rewinds)

  I checked internal numerical consistency of the v16 material against the supplied CSVs and the manuscript's own tables. The additions are, on the whole, carefully delimited: seed pairing, three-seed direct fits, four-family OOD limits, and the absence of adapter-superiority/equivalence/causality claims are all stated and respected.

  ### Concrete corrections (affect current claims or their presentation)

  1. **Trainable-parameter count is inconsistent across the manuscript and the CSV.** §1 and §4.2 state the matched partial fine-tuning updates **28,375,297** parameters; Table 6 and `parameters_and_budgets.csv` give **28,379,140** for the identically named partial-FT configuration (all six source arms in the CSV). The difference (3,843) equals the four-output target head (4,612 = LayerNorm 1,536 + Linear 768→4) minus the scalar source head (769), so the numbers can be reconciled — but as written, two different counts appear for "partial fine-tuning," and the CSV's source-arm rows match neither §4.2 nor the stated caption rule. Add one sentence stating which head each count includes, or use one count throughout. Because "identical adaptation across arms" is the central matched-design claim, this must be unambiguous.

  2. **Same estimand reported with two different intervals.** Human-minus-shuffled cation ΔRMSE is −0.120 [−0.217, −0.043] in §2.2 (10,000 draws, §4.4) but −0.120 [−0.221, −0.043] in Table 7 (5,000 draws). Identical contrast, two interval bounds, no explanation. Either reconcile (note draw counts) or report one.

  3. **Verify the censoring lower-bound arithmetic.** Using the stated inputs (67-exact pooled human RMSE 2.054; minimum IT505 error 3.76 log2), the 68-pair lower bound computes to ≈2.09, not 2.101. Per-seed values could explain this, but the derivation should be shown or the numbers reconciled, since this bound is quoted as a sensitivity result.

  ### Consistency checks that passed

  Table 6 head arithmetic is exactly right (linear 4,612; MLP 231,812; LoRA 299,524 = 12 blocks × Q,V × 2×768×8 + 4,612). Fit counts close: 363 = 165 + 198; 1,331 = 120 × 11 + 11; 93 = 30 × 3 + 3; 913 = 880 + 33; tensor accounting 195 = 128 + 2 + 65. All §2.5 prose claims about Table 5 hold against the table (including exactly the two stated scaffold exceptions). Pooled RMSE values in §2.2/§2.3 match Table 2/3 endpoint means; all quoted ΔRMSE point estimates (±0.022, −0.069, −0.121, −0.136, +0.177, −0.156, +0.306) reproduce from the tables. The −0.663/−0.664 discrepancy is rounding only.

  ### Causal interpretation

  The rewind logic is sound as stated: buffers retained, decoder refitted, arms rendered identical by full rewind, and the manuscript correctly notes rewind-all ≠ pure raw. Claims are appropriately confined to "contribution to the specified prediction loss." No violations of the declared limitations found. One residual caution worth a clause, not a correction: with three paired seeds, the seed-index bootstrap is close to enumeration over ten combinations, so direct-route intervals are even weaker than the "exploratory" label implies.

  ### Optional (outside scope)

  Crossed rather than one-to-one source×target seeds; a LoRA rank/learning-rate ablation; formal equivalence testing (TOST) only if proximity language is ever tightened; additional OOD families.
