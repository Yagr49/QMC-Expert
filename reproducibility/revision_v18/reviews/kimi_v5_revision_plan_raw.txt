• **Overall assessment.** The manuscript poses a genuine, well-motivated question — whether coarse expert binary judgements can substitute for quantitative supervision in learning molecular representations — and supports it with an unusual combination of contrastive representation learning, a head-to-head quantitative comparison, and interpretability. The arc from prior work (ref 53) to guidance learning to mechanism is compelling. My comments concern the number–code linkage, the accuracy of the "adapter" story, and sequencing of follow-up hypotheses. None undermines the core claims, but several require explicit provenance notes to remain defensible.

  **Provenance: what the v5 numbers can and cannot claim.** Recovered notebook outputs support most, not all, of Tables 1–2. In the 24 May notebook (cell 20), saved human-embedding multitask R² values match the manuscript on 7/8 endpoints; the exception is critical: the saved E. coli MIC value for the OpenAI-encoded variant is 0.5918, which rounds to 0.59, not the 0.60 printed in Table 2. Since the headline "surpasses the quantitative corpus on the hardest endpoint" claim rests on 0.60 vs 0.58, this must be traced to its cell/variant or corrected before the abstract's claim stands. The corresponding RMSEs were computed but never printed, so Table 1/2 RMSE values remain preserved original results without recovered provenance. For OOD RMSE (2.31/2.19/2.13): 2.13 derives from the later July recalculation, while the May bootstrap CSV yields 2.276 — two archival computations whose discrepancy must be disclosed, not silently resolved. Table 2's quantitative-trained embedding row comes from a checkpoint that has not been located; the archived binned-regression encoder is a different artifact and must not be presented as its source. The April seed-selection notebook (best seed 79 of 0–99) requires disclosure wherever its split touched evaluation.

  **Narrative continuity and the adapter explanation.** The adapter progression (descriptors → one-hot task labels → text-embedded class descriptions) is told inaccurately: the recovered code passes a single categorical `task_id` column to CatBoost, not one-hot indicators. Recommend keeping the original numbers (per your constraint), adding a methods footnote stating the encoding as actually implemented, publishing the four class-description strings in SI, specifying the OpenAI model/version (the old cache is absent; H1's reconstructed cell-8 vectors differ from cell-17), and letting H4 (categorical/one-hot/text/random/permuted codes) adjudicate the "semantic proximity" explanation, which is currently asserted rather than tested. Further continuity items:

  - The dataset is 1,500 labels but the encoder used 1,100 — reconcile.
  - The quantitative corpus is "~10,000 measurements" in the headline but ">18,000 entries" in §2.2 — anchor one number; "order of magnitude" is loose against 1,500.
  - Table headers give S. aureus ATCC 4300 vs ATCC 43300 in the text.
  - The Conclusion numbers conclusions First and Third around an unlabeled middle paragraph that partially duplicates §2.4.

  **Organising the hypothesis sequence.** §2.4 silently re-runs the supervision-source comparison (binned-regression ablation) at a different scale than §2.2, and the strongest causal claim is buried after the OOD narrative. Either signpost 2.2 → 2.4 explicitly, or reorder: task-adapter comparison (H2/H4) → supervision-source equivalence (H3) → OOD generalisation (H1/H2) → interpretability synthesis (H5/H6). Add an SI claim→check table mapping each v5 claim to its H-number and provenance class (preserved original / recovered output / approximate reconstruction / controlled follow-up); this satisfies your own provenance requirement and pre-empts reviewer suspicion. Two statistical points: the bootstrap behind the RMSE deltas resamples endpoint residuals while the causal analysis clusters at molecule level — align resampling to whole molecules/scaffold groups per the protocol; and the "no subpopulation" CATE conclusion rests on 17 molecules (67 exact endpoints), so state it as a bound, not an absence. Finally, reconcile CKA ≈ 0.93 ("preserved geometry") with the 15-fold silhouette gain ("reorganisation") explicitly — global preservation with local sharpening — to avoid apparent self-contradiction.
