The human embeddings are learned transformations of frozen MolFormer features. We therefore ask separately how much geometry is retained, how the quantitative decoder uses the transformed features, and whether matched label assignment or learned weights affect prediction. The archived similarity analysis gives linear CKA 0.933 in-domain and 0.946 on the new compounds. Five-fold linear probes recover human features from MolFormer with R² 0.891 ± 0.037 and MolFormer features from human embeddings with R² 0.844 ± 0.060, where ± is fold SD. These results describe shared geometry and linear recoverability, rather than an identified partition into structural and biological information.

In the archived concatenated-feature CatBoost model, summed mean absolute SHAP values on the 67 exact OOD endpoints are 2.930 for MolFormer features, 0.636 for human features and 0.761 for the task identifier. The human-to-MolFormer block ratio is 0.217. These attributions describe how that fitted model allocates prediction contributions among correlated feature blocks of different dimensions. They are neither fractions of biological variance nor evidence for a separate causal knowledge channel. Detailed archived geometry and attribution results remain in SI, together with the original conceptual diagram in Figure S34.

The stronger test of supervisory content is a comparison with shuffled labels on the same source molecules and training budget. For the compact v5 architecture, shuffled-human guidance remains competitive under grouped and OOD evaluation (Figures 6 and 9). A separate human-guided partial-backbone experiment does resolve a lower cation-held-out error than its shuffled control: ΔRMSE −0.120, conditional 95% interval [−0.217, −0.043]. The Q-HERO scaffold comparison similarly favours human over shuffled guidance (Section2.2). These results locate usefulness within particular adaptation procedures and target distributions; they do not support an architecture-independent semantic effect.

Weight interventions provide a complementary check. Restoring source encoder weights in the partial-backbone experiment raises cation-held-out RMSE by +0.109, conditional interval [+0.037, +0.201], while scaffold and four-family OOD intervals include zero. The compact weight-reset experiment does not resolve a corresponding effect and retains BatchNorm state, so it is distinct from an entirely untrained encoder. Figure S35 and Table S50 show the full contrasts for both architectures. Such interventions address dependence of the prediction procedure on learned updates, rather than a causal effect of a molecular fragment on antibacterial activity.

The evidence supports a compact, task-oriented transformation of a pretrained molecular representation whose quantitative usefulness depends on source supervision, adaptation and decoding. Close prediction scores or intervals spanning zero do not establish equivalence without an appropriate design and a justified prespecified margin. The historical residual-based causal-forest analysis likewise does not identify biological causality or equivalence to the full quantitative corpus; its protocol and limitations are retained in SI. Confirmation of what numerical information annotators saw remains necessary before assigning an independent human-knowledge interpretation to their labels.
