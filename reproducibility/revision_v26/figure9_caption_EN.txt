Figure 9. OOD prediction errors on 17 compounds from four chemical families. (a) Source-matched external compact encoders with categorical task codes: five source runs per guided arm and one raw reference. (b) Five backbone-adaptation routes, each evaluated in three runs. These are separate experiments with different decoding procedures. Each violin contains 67 exact molecule–endpoint absolute errors averaged within runs; points show individual summaries, white markers medians and bars interquartile ranges. All tails are retained. The IT505 E. coli MBC >500 mg/L bound is excluded from exact-error densities and retained separately. Human direct and Human → CB share partial-backbone source checkpoints, then use target adaptation or frozen source features with CatBoost. Neither panel uses OOD labels to train its target predictor. Correlated endpoints and four families limit inference.
