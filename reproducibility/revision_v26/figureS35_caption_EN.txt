Figure S35. Conditional prediction-loss contrasts for source-matched compact encoders and separate human-guided partial-backbone experiments. Human − shuffled compares label assignment; reset weights − intact restores source weights while retaining the experiment-specific feature-map state. Dots are ΔRMSE and lines conditional 95% paired seed/scaffold bootstrap intervals. Both panels use five source runs; compact intervals use 10,000 resamples and partial-backbone intervals 5,000. Cation/scaffold tests contain 29 scaffold clusters; OOD contains four families. Prediction models are fixed during resampling; intervals are exploratory and unadjusted. Compact resets retain BatchNorm state and are not equivalent to an untrained model. The experiments have different architectures and decoders, so contrasts are interpreted within each panel. These are computational interventions on prediction procedures, rather than causal effects on antibacterial biology.
