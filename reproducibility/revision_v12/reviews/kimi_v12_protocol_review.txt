  **Material limitations**
  - Exact-string cation matching: "including counterion variants" holds only if variants share identical cation strings; normalization is upstream and unauditable here.
  - Matching is schedule-level, not bitwise: `use_deterministic_algorithms(True, warn_only=True)` permits nondeterministic GPU ops, so arm-level numeric drift beyond the matched draws is possible.
  - Checkpoints are not standalone-reconstructable (no buffers, frozen parameters, or RNG/optimizer state); reproducibility rests entirely on replay verification.
  - Target-median AUC derives its threshold from each full target set — acceptable only as the labelled descriptive metric.
  - OOD-17 rests on a single decoder fit; no fold-level uncertainty.
  - Bootstrap machinery, archived-feature audit, and the stated contrasts live in scripts not provided; inferential claims are unaudited here.
  - Five seeds, one panel, fixed folds — exploratory and conditional, as the protocol itself concedes.
