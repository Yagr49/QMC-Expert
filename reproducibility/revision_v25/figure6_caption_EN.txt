Figure 6. Observed prediction-error distributions for matched compact supervision under (a) cation and (b) scaffold holdouts. Each vertical violin contains the same 420 compound–endpoint absolute errors, averaged across five source runs for human, quantitative and shuffled-human guidance; raw MolFormer is one fixed reference. Crossbars show medians and vertical lines the interquartile range. All points and tails are retained on shared linear axes. These are means of absolute errors, rather than errors of ensemble predictions or densities over five RMSE values. The source and decoder design is shown separately in Figure 5. Correlated endpoints and runs preclude reading density differences as significance or causal effects.
