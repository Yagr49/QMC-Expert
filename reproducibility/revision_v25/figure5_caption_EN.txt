Figure 5. Matched human and quantitative supervision of external compact encoders. Both arms use the same 681 source molecules (377 training, 304 validation), five paired source seeds, frozen MolFormer features and the 768→512→384→256 architecture, without SMOTE. Human Good/Bad labels train a contrastive encoder; continuous MIC trains a regression encoder. Source-encoder weights are frozen before separate CatBoost fits use target-training folds, identical budgets and categorical task codes. Both representations are evaluated on the same 105 QAC salts and four endpoints. This is the matched follow-up design, rather than the historical full-corpus quantitative experiment; its observed error distributions appear in Figure 6.
