Figure 5. ROC curves for Q-HERO S. aureus activity (MIC ≤ 8 µM) on 405 deduplicated molecules. (a) Fixed stratified random holdout: 324 training and 81 test molecules, including 72 active and 9 inactive test compounds. (b) Pooled predictions from five scaffold-held-out folds across 29 scaffolds. Human, quantitative and shuffled-human representations use matched partial-MolFormer adaptation on 681 source molecules excluding target salts and cations; this architecture differs from Figure 4. CatBoost classifiers are fitted independently on target-training folds under the same protocol. Curves average individual ROC curves across five paired source/decoder seeds; shading is pointwise sample SD, not a confidence interval. Raw MolFormer and dashed RF35 are fixed seed 0 references. No ensemble probabilities or test-based model selection is used. Published 421-row metrics refer to a different cohort.
