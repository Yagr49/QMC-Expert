Figure 4. Matched human and quantitative supervision of external compact encoders. (a) Frozen MolFormer embeddings are transformed by separate 768→512→384→256 encoders trained on the same 681 source molecules (377 training,304 validation) with five paired source seeds and no SMOTE. Human Good/Bad labels use the contrastive objective; continuous MIC uses regression. The learned source encoders are then applied to frozen embeddings of the105 target QAC salts. Each arm is evaluated through independently fitted CatBoost regressors under the same fixed 500-iteration/depth6/learning-rate0.05/seed42 protocol and native categorical task codes. Target labels train only the relevant target-training fold. (b,c) Cation- and scaffold-held-out errors on the same105 QAC salts and four MIC/MBC endpoints. Each vertical violin contains420 compound–endpoint mean absolute errors across five source runs; the raw MolFormer reference uses one fixed representation. Horizontal bars show medians and vertical lines interquartile ranges; all420 values are shown with decorative jitter. No OOD data or historical full-corpus quantitative Table2 checkpoint is used. These are matched follow-ups to the v5 compact architecture, not a rerun of its missing quantitative checkpoint. Different objectives limit causal attribution.
